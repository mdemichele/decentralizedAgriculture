# Decentralized Agriculture

Experiment project to test how we can use web3 to benefit small-scale organic farmers.

# Thinking

I start with a statement of the problem. Increasingly, more and more of our food system in America has become centralized. Large corporations make all of the profits. Food is shipped around the globe. Food is produced using conventional methods of fertilization and pesticide usage that destroys the soil. All of this results in a less healthy local community. A community detached from its food system. Depleted soils. Money sent out of local communities. 

I've been told that food can be a way back to community building. We as humans use food to connect with each other. We share meals. We tend gardens together. Food is the source of our health. Our original form of health care. Why can't we fix these problems?

This raises the question. How do we fix a broken food system? How do we align the goals of agriculture with the goals of ecological restoration? How do we build resilient communities? 

These questions seem to be distinct, but there is a thru line between them that I am trying to pull on. I want to figure out the role that technology plays in answering these questions. Maybe technology is not the solution to these problems, but why can't we find out?
